Why Is Blood Donation Important?

❤️ Saves Lives

Donated blood can help patients during emergencies and medical treatment.

🏥 Helps Patients

Hospitals need a safe blood supply for different medical needs.

🤝 Supports Others

Voluntary donation is one way to support people in our community.

Blood donation supports healthcare services. Blood is collected, tested and handled by trained professionals.

Eligibility and donation frequency must always be confirmed by a qualified medical professional or authorised blood bank. This student website does not assess eligibility.

About Blood Donation

What is blood donation?

Blood donation is the process of giving blood at an approved blood donation centre. The collected blood is tested and handled by trained professionals.

Who can donate?

Eligibility depends on age, health, weight, medical history and local rules. A qualified medical professional must confirm eligibility.

What are the blood groups?

The main blood groups are A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+ and O-.

Responsible use

Never rely on an online demo list to decide medical compatibility or availability. Contact an authorised service.
